# JijiSwipe Two-Friend Pilot

Use this checklist separately with two friends. Do not coach them unless they are blocked; note where they hesitate.

## Setup

- Send the friend the private join code and production link separately.
- Ask them to join, create a password, sign in, and add JijiSwipe to their iPhone home screen.
- Confirm the home-screen icon opens the app without Safari controls.

## Core journey

- Add one top and one bottom from real photos.
- Close and reopen JijiSwipe; confirm both pieces remain.
- Open one piece, rename or recategorize it, and save.
- Build and save an outfit containing at least those two pieces.
- Close and reopen JijiSwipe; confirm the outfit remains.
- Share the outfit link with you; open it while signed out.
- Revoke the link; confirm the old link stops working.
- Change the profile photo, then sign out and sign back in.

## What to record

- Any step they could not understand without help.
- Any error message, screenshot, and what they tapped immediately before it.
- Approximate cutout time and whether the result was usable.
- Their least enjoyable step and the one improvement they want most.

Gate A passes when both friends complete the journey, private data stays separated, and no blocker or data-loss bug remains.
